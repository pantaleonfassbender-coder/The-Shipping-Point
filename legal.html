Privacy notice

Controller

This site is run by the private individual named in the legal notice. Because the site can be reached from the European Economic Area, this notice is written to satisfy the General Data Protection Regulation (by way of Art. 3(2) GDPR) as well as the law of the United States. Where the GDPR applies, the operator is the controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7).

What this site is, technically

A collection of static files: pages, one script, one stylesheet, the texts as data files, and images. There is no user account, no sign-in, no newsletter and no search that sends anything. The only thing that sends data is the comment form, and only if you use it. The site sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser's storage, and uses no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising and no session recording.

Opening a page loads nothing from other servers. It uses the fonts installed on your device, and every image and data file comes from this site itself.

Server logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like every web server, Netlify's infrastructure logs the requests it answers, typically IP address, time, requested address, status, amount of data transferred, browser identification and referrer. This is technically unavoidable for delivering a website. It serves the operation and security of the service, not profiling; the operator does not analyse it. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f): the legitimate interest in a working and secure website. Retention follows Netlify's own periods.

The comment form

The page Send a comment has a form for comments on this draft. It uses Netlify Forms, the form service of the hosting provider. What you enter (the comment, and if you choose to give them your name, email address, your connection to the place and the page concerned) is sent to Netlify and stored there in the operator's account. Netlify may also record technical details of the request, such as the time, your IP address and your browser identification.

Netlify checks every submission for spam with the service Akismet (Automattic Inc., USA); for this the submission is passed to Akismet. A hidden field catches automated submissions.

The operator may receive a notification of new comments by email, so a copy can also be held in his email account (Gmail, Google).

The comments are read only by the operator and used only for the review and correction of this site and its companion piece. They are not published, not passed on and not used for any other purpose. They are deleted once they are no longer needed for that purpose; you can ask for deletion at any time at the address in the legal notice.

Sending is voluntary. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is your consent by submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.

Where data is processed

The site is run and hosted in the United States. For readers in the European Economic Area this means that request data and comments are processed outside the EEA, by the operator, by Netlify Inc. as hosting provider and form service, and by Automattic Inc. as spam filter.

Where the GDPR applies, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Please address requests to the address in the legal notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

No representative in the Union under Art. 27 has been designated.
